Another pattern with **no knee analogue**: a hard, abrupt block at the front of the ankle when the
shin travels forward over the foot, rather than the stretch you would feel from a tight calf.

The distinction is the whole point of this entry. A tight calf produces a stretch felt in the back
of the leg, and it changes depending on whether the knee is bent or straight. A block at the front
of the ankle feels like something meeting something, does not stretch, and does not care what the
knee is doing.

That difference determines what is worth trying. Calf stretching addresses the first and does
nothing for the second, which is why people who stretch diligently and see no change in their
squat are sometimes working on the wrong thing entirely.
