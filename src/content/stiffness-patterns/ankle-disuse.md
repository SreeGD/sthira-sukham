Ankles that have simply not been asked for much: little walking on varied ground, and shoes that
hold the heel above the toes for most of the day.

The calf adapts to the shortened position, dorsiflexion is given up, and the loss goes unnoticed
because flat walking needs so little. What surfaces instead is a squat where the heels lift, a
descent of stairs that feels awkward, and a knee that has quietly been taking up the slack.

Like its knee and hip equivalents, this is the most reversible of the patterns here — and the one
where the useful intervention is variety of movement rather than any particular exercise.
