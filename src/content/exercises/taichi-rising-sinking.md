  - label: Sink with the out-breath

  - label: Keep it continuous

The opening movement of most tai chi forms, and a good first thing to do with a knee that
has been still.

What it offers a stiff joint is repetition through a small range with no end-range demand at
all. The knee passes through the transition out of straight and back again, over and over,
without ever being pushed. That transition is where popliteus does its work and where many
people feel the first catch of the day.

Tying it to the breath is not decoration — it sets a pace slow enough that momentum cannot
carry the movement, which is the same principle behind everything else in this modality.
