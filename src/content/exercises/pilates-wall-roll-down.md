  - label: Restack on the way up

The wall turns a vague instruction into a concrete one. "Roll down one vertebra at a time"
is hard to feel in mid-air and obvious when your back is peeling off a flat surface.

Its place in a knee reference is the hamstrings, which attach to the pelvis and therefore
respond to what the spine and hips are doing. Rolling down with soft knees lengthens them
gently through a moving range rather than a static hold — useful for people who find held
stretches provoke a guarding response.

Soft knees are not a concession. Locked knees in a forward bend put the whole demand into
the hamstring attachment and the lower back at once.
