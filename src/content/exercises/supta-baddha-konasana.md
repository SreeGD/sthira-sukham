The seated bound angle taken to the floor, which removes the need to hold yourself upright and
makes it a position you can stay in long enough for passive tissue to respond.

The cushions under the thighs are not optional in a knee reference. In the seated version the
weight of the leg pulls the knee toward the floor with the foot fixed, and the inner knee takes
the leverage. Lying down does not change that — supporting the thigh does.

Held long and supported, this is one of the few positions that reaches the inner thigh without
asking anything of the knee at all.
