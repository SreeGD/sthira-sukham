---
name: Child's pose
traditionalName: Balasana
tradition: Hatha yoga
modality: yoga
startPosition: kneeling
targets: [rectus-femoris, vastus-intermedius, joint-capsule, gluteus-maximus]
goal: [mobility]
instructions:
  - Kneel with your knees roughly hip-width apart and your big toes touching behind you.
  - Sit your hips back toward your heels, going only as far as the knees comfortably allow.
  - Walk your hands forward and let your upper body rest down toward the floor.
  - Rest and breathe. Come out by walking the hands back and lifting the hips.
dosage: Commonly held for 30 seconds to a few minutes.
difficulty: beginner
progressions: [virasana]
regressions: [heel-slide]
contraindications:
  - Recent knee surgery without clearance
  - Meniscal injury
  - Knee pain on kneeling that padding does not resolve
  - Ankle injury that makes the foot position painful
stopIf:
  - Pain inside the knee joint rather than a gentle stretch
  - Pain at the front of the ankle
  - Numbness in the feet
equipment: [cushion, towel]
modifications:
  - Place a rolled towel behind the knees, in the crease, so the joint does not have to fold as far. This is the single most useful change for a stiff knee.
  - Put a cushion between your heels and your hips and sit on that instead of reaching your heels.
  - Take the knees wider apart, which lets the body come forward with less demand on the knee.
props:
  - A rolled towel for behind the knees.
  - A cushion or folded blanket to sit on and another under the forehead.
evidenceLabel: traditional-practice
sources: [yoga-knee-oa-meta-2024, neumann-kinesiology-2016]
---

A resting position in most yoga classes, and a deep knee bend under body weight — which
makes it something a stiff knee meets early and often struggles with.

The towel behind the knee is worth explaining because it looks like cheating and is not.
Placing something in the crease means the joint reaches its end range against padding
rather than bone on bone, and the stretch is felt through the front of the thigh where it
belongs rather than as compression inside the joint.

If the hips will not reach the heels, sitting on a cushion is the same position with the
demand reduced to what you actually have. There is no version of this pose where forcing
the knee further is the point.
