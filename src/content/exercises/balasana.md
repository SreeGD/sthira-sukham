A resting position in most yoga classes, and a deep knee bend under body weight — which
makes it something a stiff knee meets early and often struggles with.

The towel behind the knee is worth explaining because it looks like cheating and is not.
Placing something in the crease means the joint reaches its end range against padding
rather than bone on bone, and the stretch is felt through the front of the thigh where it
belongs rather than as compression inside the joint.

If the hips will not reach the heels, sitting on a cushion is the same position with the
demand reduced to what you actually have. There is no version of this pose where forcing
the knee further is the point.
