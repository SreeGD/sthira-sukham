A leg press without a leg press machine, and one of the few ways to load the quadriceps
through a full bend-to-straight range while lying down.

Lying down is what makes it useful early. The knee moves through its range under resistance
without also having to balance the body or carry it, so the load can be low and the range
generous — a combination that standing work cannot offer.

Because the band's resistance grows as it stretches, this loads the end of the push hardest,
which is the straightening range that tends to be weakest. Holding both ends of the band in
your hands means you can also simply let go, which is a genuinely useful property when a
movement turns out to be more than the knee wanted.
