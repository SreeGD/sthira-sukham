  - Stand near a wall or worktop you can touch.
  - Lift one foot slightly off the floor.

  - label: Take one foot off
    detail: Lift one foot a small way off the floor, keeping the standing knee softly bent rather
      than locked.
  - label: Let it correct
    detail: Hold the position, allowing the ankle and foot to make continuous small adjustments.
      Touch the wall if you need to.
  - label: Progress gradually
    detail: When it becomes easy, try it with eyes closed, or on a cushion, keeping support within
      reach.

The most direct way to train the thing an unreliable ankle is actually short of.

The problem after an ankle sprain is usually not strength but timing — the correcting muscles
fire a fraction late. Standing on one leg supplies a continuous stream of small corrections to
make, which is the demand that trains timing rather than force.

That is why the wobble is not a fault to eliminate. An ankle held rigid is an ankle avoiding the
exercise. Gripping with the toes does the same thing more subtly: it locks the foot and hands the
correcting job to the hip.
