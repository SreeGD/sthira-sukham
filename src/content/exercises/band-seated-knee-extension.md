Straightforward quadriceps strengthening through a range, with the load easy to adjust by
changing the band or where you sit.

One honest caution about this specific movement. Seated knee extension loads the kneecap
against its groove throughout the middle of the range, and for a knee that is already
irritated at the front, that can be exactly the wrong place to work. If the front of your
knee is the problem, the standing banded version and the closed-chain work — step-downs,
footwork, wall sits — are usually better tolerated.

Where the quadriceps is simply weak and the kneecap is not the issue, this is one of the
easiest ways to load it with equipment that fits in a drawer.
