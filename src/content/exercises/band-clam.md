---
name: Banded clam
traditionalName: Clam with resistance
tradition: Pilates (mat repertoire, loaded variation)
modality: pilates
startPosition: side-lying
targets:
  - gluteus-medius
  - gluteus-minimus
  - tensor-fasciae-latae
goal:
  - strength
quickSteps:
  - Band above both knees. Lie on your side.
  - Hips stacked, knees bent, heels together.
  - Lift the top knee against the band.
  - Lower slowly.
keyPoint: Use the lightest band you have. Resistance makes cheating far easier.
feelItIn: The side of the top hip, quickly.
commonMistake: Rolling the pelvis back, which the band makes much more tempting.
instructions:
  - label: Position the band
    detail: Place a looped resistance band around both thighs, just above the knees.
  - label: Lie on your side
    detail: Lie on your side with one hip directly above the other, knees bent to about a right
      angle, heels in line with your back.
  - label: Lift against the band
    detail: Keeping the heels together and the pelvis completely still, lift the top knee against
      the band.
  - label: Lower under control
    detail: Lower slowly under control. Stop before the pelvis begins to roll backward. Complete
      your repetitions on this side, then roll over and repeat with the other leg on top.
dosage:
  reps: 10 to 15 repetitions per side
  sets: 1 to 3 sets per session
  frequency: Once daily
  note: Commonly described as 10 to 15 repetitions per side, one to two sets.
difficulty: intermediate
regressions:
  - pilates-clam
progressions:
  - band-lateral-walk
contraindications:
  - Recent hip surgery without clearance
  - Pinching at the front or side of the hip
stopIf:
  - Pinching at the side of the hip
  - Low back pain
  - The pelvis rolls backward and you cannot stop it
equipment:
  - resistance-band
modifications:
  - Use the lightest band you have. Resistance makes rolling the pelvis far more tempting, and a
    rolled pelvis undoes the exercise entirely.
  - Reduce the range before reducing the band. A small honest lift beats a large compensated one.
  - Keep a hand on your top hip bone so you feel the roll starting.
props:
  - A cushion under the head, and a light looped band.

The clam with resistance added, and the point at which the exercise gets much easier to do
badly.

A band rewards cheating. The pelvis rolling backward lets bigger muscles at the front of the
hip contribute, the knee travels further, and the set feels more productive while doing less
of what it was for. That is why the guidance here is to shorten the range rather than drop
the band, and to keep a hand on the hip bone.

Where the unloaded clam has become genuinely easy at a full honest range, this is the next
step. Where it has not, it is not.
