      travel without the opposite hip lifting.
  - label: Switch sides
    detail: Lift the knees back to the middle and lower them to the other side, moving under control
      rather than swinging.
  - label: Keep the chest up
    detail: Sit as tall as you can throughout, on a cushion if that helps, so the movement stays at
      the hips.

Hip rotation is the direction most reliably lost and least often trained, because nothing in a
sedentary day asks for it.

This position asks for both directions at once: one hip rotates outward while the other rotates
inward. That is efficient, and it also makes the asymmetry obvious — most people find one side
noticeably freer than the other.

The knee caution matters here more than in most positions. When the hip will not rotate far
enough, the shin becomes a lever and the knee absorbs the difference. A twisting sensation at the
knee means the range has come from the wrong joint, and the answer is a smaller range, not more
effort.
