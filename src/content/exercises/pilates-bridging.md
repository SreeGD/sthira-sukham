The same shape as the yoga bridge with a different intention, and the difference is
instructive. The yoga version holds a position; this one is about the sequence of getting
there — articulating the spine one segment at a time in both directions.

That segmental control is the Pilates emphasis, and it makes the exercise harder while making
it lighter. Peeling down one part at a time is considerably more demanding than simply
lowering, and it exposes the places where the spine moves as a block rather than a chain.

For the knee, the relevant outcome is the same as the yoga bridge: a gluteus maximus that
extends the hip is a gluteus maximus that is not leaving the job to the hamstrings.
