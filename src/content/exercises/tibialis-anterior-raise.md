---
name: Toe raises
modality: clinical-rom
startPosition: standing-supported
targets:
  - tibialis-anterior
goal:
  - strength
quickSteps:
  - Stand with heels a little way from a wall.
  - Lean your back lightly against it.
  - Lift the toes and forefeet off the floor.
  - Lower slowly.
keyPoint: Lower slowly. Dropping the feet back down skips most of the work.
feelItIn: The front of the shin, working and often burning quickly.
commonMistake: Rocking backward from the hips to lift the toes higher instead of using the shin.
instructions:
  - label: Get into position
    detail: Stand with your back against a wall and your heels roughly a hand's width away from it.
  - label: Settle your weight
    detail: Let your back rest lightly on the wall so you are not relying on balance.
  - label: Lift the toes
    detail: Keeping your heels down, lift your toes and the balls of your feet off the floor as far
      as they will go.
  - label: Lower under control
    detail: Lower slowly rather than letting the feet drop, and pause before the next repetition.
dosage:
  reps: 15 to 20 repetitions
  sets: 1 to 3 sets per session
  frequency: Once daily
  note: Commonly described as the counterpart to calf raises.
difficulty: beginner
progressions:
  - single-leg-balance
contraindications:
  - Acute pain at the front of the shin
  - Recent ankle or lower leg fracture without clearance
stopIf:
  - Sharp pain along the front of the shin rather than a working burn
  - Numbness or pins and needles in the foot
  - Cramping that does not settle
equipment:
  - wall
evidenceLabel: mechanistic-rationale
sources:
  - neumann-kinesiology-2016
laterality: bilateral
---

Everyone does calf raises; almost nobody does the opposite, and the imbalance shows.

The calf shortens and strengthens with everyday use and footwear, while the muscle on the front
of the shin quietly weakens. That combination is behind a good deal of the ankle stiffness people
attribute to age, and it is behind catching a toe on a kerb.

The muscle fatigues quickly, which surprises people. Burning after fifteen repetitions is normal
and is the point.
