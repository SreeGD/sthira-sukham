name: Straight-leg raise
modality: clinical-rom
startPosition: supine
targets:
  - rectus-femoris
  - vastus-intermedius
  - iliopsoas
goal:
  - strength
  - motor-control
quickSteps:
  - Lie down, other knee bent, foot flat.
  - Lock the working knee straight first.
  - Lift the straight leg to knee height.
  - Lower slowly.
keyPoint: Lock the knee before you lift. Lifting first makes it a hip exercise.
feelItIn: The front of the thigh, working hard.
commonMistake: The knee sagging into a bend partway up. Lower the height instead.
instructions:
  - label: Get into position
    detail: Lie on your back with the working leg straight and the other knee bent, foot flat on the
      floor.
  - label: Lock the knee first
    detail: Tighten the thigh muscle first and lock the knee straight — this comes before any lifting.
  - label: Raise the straight leg
    detail: Keeping the knee locked, raise the straight leg to about the height of the opposite knee.
  - label: Lower under control
    detail: Lower slowly. If the knee bends on the way up or down, the lift is too high.
dosage:
  reps: 10 to 15 repetitions
  sets: 1 to 3 sets per session
  frequency: Once daily
  note: Commonly described as 10 to 15 repetitions, one to three sets, once daily.

  - pilates-single-leg-bridge
contraindications:
  - Acute low back pain that this position reproduces
  - Recent hip or abdominal surgery without clearance
  - Inability to fully straighten the knee — work on that first
stopIf:
  - Low back pain during the lift
  - The knee sags into a bend and you cannot correct it
  - Hip flexor cramping or pinching at the front of the hip

  - none

The step up from a quad set: the same locked-straight knee, now carrying the weight of the
leg.

The sequence is what makes it work. Lock the knee first, then lift. Lifting first and
locking second turns it into a hip flexor exercise with the quadriceps along for the ride,
which is not what it is for.

A knee that sags into a bend partway through is telling you the quadriceps has run out
before the hip has. That is useful information: lower the height rather than fighting it.
