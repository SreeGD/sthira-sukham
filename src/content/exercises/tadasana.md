The least eventful pose in yoga and, for a knee that habitually locks backward, one of the
more useful.

Many people stand with the knees pushed past straight into hyperextension. It feels stable
because it is — the joint is resting on its own end range rather than on muscle. The cost is
that the ligaments at the back take the load all day and the quadriceps learns it is not
needed.

Firming the thigh without locking is the whole instruction. It is a small, unglamorous
correction, and it changes what several hours of standing does to a knee.
