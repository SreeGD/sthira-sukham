A hip opener done lying down, with the floor holding the pelvis so the stretch cannot quietly
migrate into the lower back.

For a stiff knee the thing to notice is that this asks for a lot of knee flexion as well as
hip movement. If your knee does not comfortably reach a full bend yet, holding behind the
thighs instead of the feet gives you the hip stretch with far less demand on the joint.

Reaching the feet is the part most people force. A strap removes the reason to, and the pose
works exactly as well.
