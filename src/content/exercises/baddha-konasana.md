A hip opener that gets misapplied to the knee more than almost any other position.

The intent is a stretch through the inner thigh and a rotation at the hip. When the hip
will not give, the shin becomes a long lever and the strain arrives at the inner knee
instead — which is why pressing the knees down with the hands is specifically discouraged
here. Weight hanging on an unsupported knee does the same thing more slowly.

Supporting the knees on cushions is not a lesser version. It removes the leverage and
leaves the stretch where it was meant to be.

For a knee reference the relevance is indirect: adductors that will not lengthen keep the
thigh drawn inward, and a knee that collapses toward the midline under load is loading its
inner side unevenly.
