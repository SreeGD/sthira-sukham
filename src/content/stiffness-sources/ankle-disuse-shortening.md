clinicalTerm: Disuse shortening
plainLanguageGloss: Tissue has adapted to the range you actually use

Shoes with any heel, and days that involve little walking on varied ground, both hold the ankle in a shortened position. The calf adapts, dorsiflexion is given up, and every movement that needs the shin to travel forward over the foot starts being achieved some other way.
