Fluid in the hip is harder to see than at the knee — the joint sits deep, wrapped in muscle, so there is no visible swelling to notice. What you get instead is a feeling of fullness or pressure deep in the groin, and a hip that finds one position more comfortable than any other and resists leaving it.
