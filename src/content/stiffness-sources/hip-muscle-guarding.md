The hip is surrounded by large, strong muscles, which makes guarding here more effective and more limiting than at the knee. When the nervous system reads the joint as vulnerable, those muscles raise their resting tension and the range shrinks before anything reaches the end of it.

Guarding eases as you warm up and varies between days — which is how you tell it from the capsule.
