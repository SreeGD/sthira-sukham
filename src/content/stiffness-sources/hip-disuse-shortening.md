A sedentary day holds the hip flexed and rotated in almost no direction at all. The tissue adapts to that as its working range, and extension and rotation are relinquished — not through damage, but because nothing asked for them.

This is the most common and most reversible source of hip stiffness, and the one most often mistaken for something worse.
