The hip capsule is thick and strong — it has to be, holding a ball in a socket that carries your weight. When it tightens it restricts in a firm, consistent way that does not ease with warming up, and it typically takes rotation before it takes flexion.

That order matters. Rotation is the direction nobody misses until they need it, so capsular tightening at the hip is often well advanced before anything feels wrong.
