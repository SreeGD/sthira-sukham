Hip osteoarthritis characteristically takes rotation first, then extension, then flexion — a recognised order rather than a general loss. Pain is often felt in the groin rather than over the hip itself, and sometimes referred to the knee, which is why a knee complaint occasionally turns out to be a hip.

As with the knee, exercise is first-line management in current guidance rather than something to be careful of.
