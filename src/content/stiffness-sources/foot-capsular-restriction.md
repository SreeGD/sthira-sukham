At the foot this almost always means the big toe joint. The capsule tightens, the toe stops bending upward as far, and because walking asks for that motion on every step the loss is noticed constantly rather than at the extremes.

The end feel is firm and arrives at the same point each time. What distinguishes it from a tight big-toe flexor is that bending the ankle makes no difference to it — the flexor runs up behind the inner ankle, so slackening it by pointing the foot gives a tight *muscle* more room and gives a tight *capsule* none.
