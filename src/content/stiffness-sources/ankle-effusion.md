Ankle swelling is highly visible, and it restricts by occupying space the joint needs to move through. A swollen ankle typically loses dorsiflexion first, because that is the direction with the least room to spare.

Swelling that is warm and red rather than simply puffy is a different matter and belongs on the list of reasons to stop and see someone.
