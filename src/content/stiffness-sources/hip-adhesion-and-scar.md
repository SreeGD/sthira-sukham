After hip surgery or a long period of immobility, healing tissue can bind layers that should slide over one another. The result sits at a fixed point in the range and does not shift with warming up.

If you have had hip surgery, your surgeon or physiotherapist may have set specific limits — particularly on rotation and on bringing the leg across the body. Those instructions are about your hip and your procedure, and take precedence over anything general.
