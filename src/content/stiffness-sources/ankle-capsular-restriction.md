A tightened ankle capsule restricts dorsiflexion with a firm, springy end feel that arrives at the same point every time. It is common after any period in a boot or cast, where the joint is held still for weeks.

Unlike a tight calf, it does not change with the knee bent or straight — which is a useful way to tell the two apart.
