Descending stairs is the single most common thing people with stiff or painful knees say they
have stopped doing properly.

It asks for two different things at once. The knee has to bend to 90–120° to clear each step,
and the quadriceps has to lengthen while working to lower your body weight onto the step
below. That second demand — controlled lowering — is where most people are short, and it is
not trained by going up.

The hip matters more here than people expect. If the pelvis drops or the thigh rolls inward
on each step, the kneecap's groove moves underneath it, which is why the movements below
include hip work as well as thigh work.
