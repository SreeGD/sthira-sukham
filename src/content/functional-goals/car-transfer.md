A car seat is low, the doorway is narrow, and the movement asks the leg to bend and rotate at
the same time — which is why it catches out knees that manage stairs fine.

The rotation is the part people miss. If the hip will not turn, the twist has to come from
somewhere, and the knee has very little rotation to give. So the useful work is as much about
hip mobility as about knee bend.
