Getting to the floor and back is the activity that most reliably disappears first and gets
noticed last — usually at the point where something needs picking up.

It demands range rather than strength: a deep fold at the knee, length through the front of
the thigh, and enough ankle movement to keep the heels down. Where any one of those is short,
the others cannot compensate.

Work here is patient work. Range at the far end of the joint responds to being visited
gently and often, not to being forced once.
