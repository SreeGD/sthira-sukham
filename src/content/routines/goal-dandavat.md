---
title: Preparing for dandavat — 30 minutes
purpose: >-
  A half-hour session for repeated prostrations: the deep fold the knee passes through, the
  controlled descent the thigh has to manage, and the ankle range the position needs.
approxDurationMinutes: 30
equipment: [none, wall, cushion, towel, strap, chair]
order: 170
goal: dandavat
weeklyPlan: >-
  Split it if you can. The range work — heel slides and the quadriceps stretch — repays being
  done on several days a week; the strength half can stay weekly. Repetition tolerance builds
  from doing a little often, not from one long session.
steps:
  - exercise: heel-slide
    note: 5 minutes. Open the deep bend with nothing to carry.
  - exercise: prone-quad-stretch
    note: 4 minutes. Length at the front of the thigh, hip straight.
  - exercise: bent-knee-calf-stretch
    note: 3 minutes. Ankle range, so the knee does not absorb the difference.
  - exercise: balasana
    note: 4 minutes. The fold under body weight, padded and supported.
  - exercise: taichi-slow-flexion-extension
    note: 5 minutes. Controlled lowering, which is the descent in miniature.
  - exercise: setu-bandha
    note: 4 minutes. Glutes for the return to standing.
  - exercise: step-down
    note: 5 minutes. Repeated controlled lowering under load.
orderRationale: >-
  Range before load, and lowering before rising. The first three steps make the deep fold
  available; child's pose introduces body weight into it with an escape route; the last three
  build the controlled descent and the push back up. Descent work is weighted more heavily than
  rising because lowering under control is the harder half and the one that fails first when the
  repetitions add up.
sources: [rowe-knee-functional-rom-2000, neumann-kinesiology-2016, cochrane-exercise-knee-oa-2024]
---

Prepares for the physical demand rather than the practice itself, which is not this reference's
business.

Two adjustments outside this session do more than anything inside it. A folded mat or cloth
under the knees removes the hard-floor contact that causes much of the discomfort. And lowering
by way of a kneeling position, with the hands taking some of the weight, asks considerably less
of the knee than dropping straight down — while changing nothing about the offering.
