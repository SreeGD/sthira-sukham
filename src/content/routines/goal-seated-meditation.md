---
title: Sitting comfortably — 30 minutes
purpose: >-
  A half-hour session aimed almost entirely at hip rotation, so that sitting cross-legged does
  not ask the knee to supply a turn it does not have.
approxDurationMinutes: 30
equipment: [none, cushion, strap, chair, towel]
order: 180
goal: seated-meditation
weeklyPlan: >-
  Hip rotation responds to frequency. Ten minutes of the supported positions on most days will
  change your sitting more than one weekly session, and they are restful enough to do in the
  evening.
steps:
  - exercise: supta-baddha-konasana
    note: 6 minutes. Supported, no load on the knee. Cushions under both thighs.
  - exercise: ananda-balasana
    note: 4 minutes. Hip width and rotation with the back flat on the floor.
  - exercise: taichi-waist-rotation
    note: 3 minutes. Rotation standing, knees turning with the hips.
  - exercise: eka-pada-rajakapotasana
    note: 5 minutes. Only the supported version, hip well propped. Skip if any knee pain.
  - exercise: baddha-konasana
    note: 5 minutes. Sitting up on height, knees resting on cushions.
  - exercise: anjaneyasana
    note: 4 minutes. The front of the hip, which long sitting also shortens.
  - exercise: viparita-karani
    note: 3 minutes. Finish with the legs resting up a wall.
orderRationale: >-
  Lying before sitting, supported before loaded. Every early step opens hip rotation with the
  knee carrying nothing, so that by the time you sit cross-legged the range is available from the
  hip. Doing it the other way round — sitting first and hoping the hips open — is how the knee
  ends up as the joint that gives way.
sources: [neumann-kinesiology-2016, yoga-knee-oa-meta-2024]
---

Almost none of this is knee work, and that is the finding rather than an omission.

Cross-legged sitting needs the thigh to rotate outward at the hip. When the hip will not, the
shin becomes a lever and the knee takes rotation it barely has — held for an hour, that is a slow
strain noticed afterwards rather than at the time.

The two changes that matter most are not exercises at all: sit higher than feels necessary, and
support the knees. Both let the position be held for hours instead of minutes.
