---
title: Getting in and out of a car — 30 minutes
purpose: >-
  A half-hour session for the bend-and-rotate combination a car seat asks for, worked mostly at
  the hip so the knee is not the joint supplying the turn.
approxDurationMinutes: 30
equipment: [none, wall, cushion, strap, chair]
order: 130
goal: car-transfer
weeklyPlan: >-
  Twice weekly if you can. This is a mobility-led session, and mobility responds to frequency
  more than to duration — two twenty-minute sessions beat one of forty.
steps:
  - exercise: heel-slide
    note: 4 minutes. Open the bend first.
  - exercise: taichi-waist-rotation
    note: 4 minutes. Rotation from the hip, with the knees turning too.
  - exercise: supta-baddha-konasana
    note: 5 minutes. Supported hip opening, no load on the knee at all.
  - exercise: ananda-balasana
    note: 4 minutes. Hip width and rotation, lying down.
  - exercise: pilates-side-kick
    note: 4 minutes. Hip moving independently of the pelvis.
  - exercise: baddha-konasana
    note: 4 minutes. Sitting, knees supported on cushions.
  - exercise: taichi-weight-shifting
    note: 5 minutes. Transfer weight slowly — the swing out of a car is one of these.
orderRationale: >-
  Almost all of this is hip work, and that is the point. The knee has very little rotation to
  give, so if the hip cannot turn, the twist arrives at the knee. Rotation drills come early
  while the joint is fresh; the supported positions sit in the middle where time under gentle
  load does the work; weight shifting closes because getting out of a car is a loaded transfer,
  not a stretch.
sources: [rowe-knee-functional-rom-2000, neumann-kinesiology-2016]
---

A car seat is low, the doorway is narrow, and the movement bends and rotates at once — which is
why it catches out knees that manage stairs perfectly well.

If any step here produces a twisting sensation inside the knee rather than a stretch at the hip,
that is the signal to stop and reduce the range, not to work through it.
