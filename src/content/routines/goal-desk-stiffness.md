---
title: Desk-worker reset — 30 minutes
purpose: >-
  A half-hour session undoing what a working day does: length at the front of the hip and the
  back of the ankle, and glutes that switch back on when you stand.
approxDurationMinutes: 30
equipment: [none, wall, chair, cushion, towel]
order: 140
goal: desk-stiffness
weeklyPlan: >-
  This one genuinely benefits from being broken up. The full session weekly, plus two minutes of
  the low lunge on any day you have been sitting for hours, is worth more than the session on
  its own.
steps:
  - exercise: anjaneyasana
    note: 5 minutes. The direct answer to hours of hip flexion. Pad the back knee.
  - exercise: bent-knee-calf-stretch
    note: 4 minutes. The other end of the sitting problem.
  - exercise: straight-leg-calf-stretch
    note: 3 minutes. The upper calf, which the bent-knee version misses.
  - exercise: pilates-clam
    note: 4 minutes. Waking the side of the hip after a day switched off.
  - exercise: setu-bandha
    note: 4 minutes. Hip extension with the glutes, not the hamstrings.
  - exercise: pilates-swimming
    note: 4 minutes. Hip extension with the hip already straight.
  - exercise: taichi-knee-over-toe
    note: 3 minutes. Check the pattern under load before you walk off.
  - exercise: tadasana
    note: 3 minutes. Standing without locking the knees back.
orderRationale: >-
  Lengthen what shortened, then switch on what went quiet, then check the result standing. The
  order matters because a glute asked to work through a range the hip flexor will not give
  simply cannot, and the session would train compensation instead. Standing checks come last
  because they are where the change either shows up or does not.
sources: [neumann-kinesiology-2016, cochrane-exercise-knee-oa-2024]
---

The pattern with no injury and no diagnosis — the knee simply feels tight, and worst on standing
up.

Almost nothing here is aimed at the knee, which is the honest answer for this goal. The hip
flexors and the calf are where sitting does its work, and the knee is reporting a problem it
did not create.
