The arch of the foot is not held up by ligament alone; tibialis posterior is its main active
support, and its tendon runs behind the bony bump on the inside of the ankle.

The reason it belongs in a knee-and-hip reference is the chain running upward. A foot that rolls
inward takes the shin bone with it, because the two are mechanically coupled. The knee then finds
itself loaded at an angle, and the thigh above rotates to accommodate.

That is the whole lower-limb chain in one structure: a muscle in the calf changing what the knee
experiences, without touching it.
