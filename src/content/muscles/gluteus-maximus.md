The most powerful hip extensor, and the muscle most reliably underused in people who sit for
a living.

Its connection to the knee is indirect but real, running through two routes. Part of it
inserts into the iliotibial band, giving it a direct line to the outer knee. And when it is
not doing its share of hip extension, the hamstrings absorb the difference — which matters
because the hamstrings also cross the knee, and a hamstring kept perpetually busy is a
hamstring that resists full knee extension.
