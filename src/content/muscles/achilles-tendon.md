The strongest tendon in the body, and the one people are most likely to have heard of going
wrong.

For stiffness purposes the important thing is what it is: dense tendon, not muscle. It transmits
force, it stores and returns energy, and it does not meaningfully lengthen under any stretch you
can apply to yourself. When calf stretching changes your ankle range, what changed was the muscle
above.

A sudden pop at the back of the ankle with sudden weakness on push-off is on the list of reasons
to stop and see someone — it is sometimes surprisingly painless, which is why it gets ignored.
