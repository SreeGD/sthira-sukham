The IT band is included here mainly to correct a common misunderstanding about it.

It is frequently treated as a muscle to be stretched or a tight structure to be released. It
is neither. It is dense connective tissue with enormous tensile strength, and the amount it
lengthens under any force a person can apply to themselves is negligible.

What genuinely varies is the tension running through it — and that comes from above, from
tensor fasciae latae and gluteus maximus, both of which insert into it. A TFL working
overtime to compensate for a weak gluteus medius keeps the band taut, and the outer knee
feels the result.

So the band is a messenger. The useful work is at the hip, which is where the message
originates.
