Broad and flat where semitendinosus is cord-like, and sitting underneath it.

Its notable feature is an attachment into the posterior joint capsule. As the knee bends,
semimembranosus draws that capsule backward and out of the way, so the joint does not pinch
its own lining. Together with vastus intermedius doing the same job at the front, it is part
of a small system for keeping soft tissue clear of a closing hinge.
