The retinaculum is what keeps the kneecap tracking where it should, and it is passive — it
restrains, it does not pull.

The medial and lateral sheets are meant to balance. Where the lateral side is tighter, the
kneecap sits slightly outward and resists being moved inward, which alters how it loads its
groove throughout the range.

Because this is passive tissue, no amount of strengthening addresses it directly. What
addresses it is moving the kneecap itself — which is why patellar mobilisation exists as a
distinct thing rather than as a variation on a quadriceps exercise.
