Sitting deepest of the three glutes, minimus is essentially a partner to gluteus medius —
same job, smaller share, and it is not usefully trained in isolation.

It is catalogued separately because the muscle that stabilises your hip in single-leg stance
is genuinely two muscles, and exercises addressing one address both.
