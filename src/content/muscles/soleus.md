Soleus lies beneath gastrocnemius and, unlike it, does not cross the knee — it runs from the
shin bones to the heel. That anatomical detail is why it is tested and stretched with the
knee bent, which takes gastrocnemius out of the picture and leaves soleus alone.

It is included in a knee reference because its restriction is functionally a knee problem
even though it is anatomically an ankle one. Most positions where a stiff knee is noticed —
squatting, sitting down, going downstairs — are bent-knee positions, and bent-knee ankle
range is soleus's territory.
