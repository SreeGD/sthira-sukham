The band under the sole that turns a flexible foot into a rigid lever at the moment you push off.

Its connection to the calf is the part worth knowing. The Achilles tendon and the plantar fascia
are effectively continuous around the heel bone, so tension in one is felt by the other. That is
why calf tightness and heel pain travel together, and why calf work is the standard first
approach to a sore heel.

The morning pattern is characteristic: worst on the first few steps after rest, easing within a
few minutes, returning after sitting.
