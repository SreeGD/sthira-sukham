The largest of the four quadriceps heads. Its line of pull runs slightly outward, which is
balanced in a healthy knee by the inner head pulling the other way.

That balance is the interesting part. Vastus lateralis is generally the more robust of the
pair and the less likely to shut down after swelling, so when the inner head is inhibited
the outward pull is left with less opposing it. This is the mechanism behind a good deal of
the discussion about kneecap tracking.
