The capsule is passive tissue, which changes what can be done about it. It cannot be
switched on, and it does not respond to strengthening.

It does respond to being moved. Tissue held still for a long period tightens; tissue taken
through its range regularly stays pliable. This is why the general advice for a stiff knee is
so often "keep moving it" rather than anything more specific — for the capsule, that is
genuinely the mechanism.

Capsular restriction has a distinguishing feel worth learning: firm, springy, arriving at the
same point every time, and not much improved by warming up. Muscular restriction eases as you
move. Capsular restriction largely does not.
