The quiet one. Vastus intermedius lies directly against the femur underneath rectus
femoris, so it cannot be seen or palpated, and it gets correspondingly little attention.

It earns a mention here for its deep fibres, which attach to the joint capsule and pull it
clear as the knee straightens — stopping the capsule from being pinched in the joint. It is
a small job that is only noticeable when it stops happening.
