Five muscles running along the inner thigh, of which only gracilis actually reaches past the
knee. The group nonetheless matters here because of where it puts the thigh.

Their relevance to knee stiffness is positional. Squatting and deep knee bending require the
thighs to travel outward as well as the knees to fold. Adductors that will not lengthen make
that unavailable, and the movement gets achieved by letting the knees fall inward instead —
which is felt as knee stiffness, and is really hip restriction.
