  achieves little when the pull originates at the hip.

A small muscle with reach well beyond its size, because it does not act on bone directly so
much as tension a long connective band that does.

Its clinical interest is almost entirely about compensation. TFL and gluteus medius share
responsibility for stabilising the pelvis in single-leg stance. When the glute is not
holding up its end, TFL takes on more, stays busy, and keeps the IT band under tension — and
the consequence gets felt at the outer knee, a long way from the cause.
